Overview

Project Aurora is designed to create a single, unified interface for high-value AI capabilities. It abstracts the complexity of interacting with various models (GPT, Claude, Gemma), providers (Ollama, custom endpoints), and technologies like MCPs and agents. Our goal is to provide enterprises with an easy-to-adopt gateway, resulting in strong hiring value by proving mastery across diverse modern tech stacks.

By centralizing access and enforcing robust governance within a Docker/Kubernetes ecosystem, we manage risk while maximizing feature velocity. This view page serves as the primary single source of truth for all stakeholders regarding the project's health and roadmap.
